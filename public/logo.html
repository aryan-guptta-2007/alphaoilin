Usage

Do

  • Full colour on white or light grey backgrounds
  • White version on the dark header and footer
  • Simplified version below 40px — favicon, app icon, stamps
  • Single-colour ink version for fax, invoices and one-colour print
  • Keep clear space equal to the width of the orange droplet on all sides

Don't

  • Place the full-colour mark on a photograph or coloured panel
  • Stretch it — the height-to-width ratio is fixed
  • Recolour the gradients or swap the blue, green and orange order
  • Add shadows, outlines or bevels
  • Use the full detailed version below 40px; the blue tail disappears
